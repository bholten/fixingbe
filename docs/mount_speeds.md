# Mount Speeds

This document descibes movement speeds. Different creatures have different physical movement speeds in game and it may have something to do with the creature level calculation.

Source: https://www.swgemu.com/archive/scrapbookv51/data/20071217133940/index.html

**Other** creatures also had different movement speeds, but I can find no source for them. We can probably use our best judgment (Woolamander, Rancor = Slow, Gurreck and other cats = fast).

## Guide

I've listed the mounts by their general type, because each species within a given type perform in the same way. For example, a Cu pa moves in the same was as does the Wasteland Cu pa.

Vehicles

1000 (fast), 500 (slow): AV-21, Speederbike Swoop
882 (fast), 440 (slow): Speederbike, Flashspeeder
765 (fast), 383 (slow): X34, XP-38, V-35, AB-1 Landspeeders
647 (fast), 323 (slow): X31 Landspeeder

Notice that a fast galloper isn't necessarily going to be a fast runner, or walker.
An example of how to put this data to use would be in the planning of a caravan event. In a caravan, you want the group to move at a rate that doesn't feel too slow, but that is slow enough to allow the scouts to comfortably run ahead. If the leader were to ride a walking Carrion Spat, Kaadu, Humbaba, Brackaset, or Falumpaset, then scouts can ride their choice of scout mounts.
